# Privacy Policy

**App:** QK Allen Book Poster
**Operator:** Brent Allen
**Contact:** brent@qbenterprisegroup.com
**Effective date:** October 24, 2026

## Summary

QK Allen Book Poster is a private automation tool. It publishes promotional posts for books written under the pen names QK Allen and Kenya Ayre to social media accounts owned by the operator. It is not offered to the public, it has no users other than the operator, and it does not collect information about anyone else.

## Who uses this app

Only the operator. The app connects exclusively to social media accounts that the operator owns or manages:

- Facebook Pages (QK Allen Books, Kenya Ayre Books)
- Instagram professional accounts
- X (Twitter) accounts
- TikTok accounts

No other person can sign in to, authorize, or use the app.

## Information the app handles

When the operator authorizes the app on a platform, the platform issues **access tokens** that let the app post to the operator's own accounts. The app also handles the operator's account identifiers (such as user ID, page ID, and username) and the content it publishes: book poster images, post text, and links to the books on Amazon.

The app does **not**:

- collect, read, or store information about other people, followers, or commenters;
- read private messages;
- track visitors or use cookies, analytics, or advertising identifiers;
- sell, rent, or share any information with third parties.

## How information is stored

Access tokens and account identifiers are stored only on the operator's own computer, in files readable only by the operator's user account. A local database on the same computer records which posts were published, when, and the resulting post links. Nothing is stored on any external server operated by the app.

Poster images may be uploaded temporarily to a public image host when a platform requires a public image URL (for example, Instagram). These are the operator's own promotional images, intended for public posting.

## How information is used

Information is used only to publish the operator's promotional posts to the operator's own accounts, and to record the outcome of each post for troubleshooting.

## Third-party platforms

The app uses the official APIs of Meta (Facebook and Instagram), X, and TikTok. Content published through the app is subject to each platform's own privacy policy and terms.

## Data retention

Tokens are kept until the operator revokes the app's access or removes the files. Post records are kept for the operator's reference and can be deleted at any time.

## Data deletion

To delete all data held by this app:

1. **Revoke access on each platform.**
   - Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → remove the app.
   - Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → remove the app.
   - X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps → revoke access.
   - TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions → remove the app.
2. **Delete local data.** The operator deletes the token files and the local post database from their computer.

Because the app has no users other than the operator, no other person's data exists to delete. If you believe this app holds data about you, contact brent@qbenterprisegroup.com and it will be reviewed and deleted within 30 days.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process any information about children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
